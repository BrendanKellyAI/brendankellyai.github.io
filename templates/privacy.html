Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Article 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Article 16);
  • have your data erased (Article 17);
  • restrict how it is used (Article 18);
  • receive it in a portable form (Article 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21);
    • {% if analytics %}
  • withdraw your consent to analytics at any time, without affecting what happened before (Article 7(3)), with Cookie settings at the foot of any page.
    • {% endif %}

To use any of these rights, email {{ site.privacy_email }}. You will get a reply within one month, as the GDPR requires (Article 12(3)). There is no charge.

{% if analytics %}

Analytics data is linked only to a random identifier in your browser's cookie, not to your name, so the quickest way to stop it and remove the cookies is to decline in Cookie settings.

{% endif %}
